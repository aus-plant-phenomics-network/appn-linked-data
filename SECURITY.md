# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Do not open a public issue for a security problem.**

Report privately through GitHub's [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/aus-plant-phenomics-network/appn-linked-data/security/advisories/new)
(Security tab → Report a vulnerability), or email **{{security contact address}}**.

Please include:

- what the issue is and where it occurs,
- the steps or input needed to reproduce it,
- the impact you believe it has,
- any suggested remediation.

## What to expect

| Stage | Target |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgement of your report | 3 business days |
| Initial assessment and severity | 1 week |
| Fix for critical severity on a deployed service | 48 hours |
| Fix for other confirmed issues | Next scheduled release |

We will keep you updated as the assessment progresses, and will credit you in
the advisory unless you ask us not to.
